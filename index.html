What is Mochi?

✿

A soft and chewy rice cake

Mochi is a traditional Japanese rice cake made from glutinous rice or sweet rice flour. It is known for its soft, stretchy and chewy texture.

Mochi can be enjoyed plain or filled with sweet treats such as red bean paste, fruit or ice cream. It is often shared during celebrations and special occasions in Japan.

A little treat with a big history! 🌸
Get everything ready

Ingredients

Simple ingredients for a small batch of soft mochi.

  • Glutinous rice flour1 cup
  • Sugar¼ cup
  • Water¾ cup
  • Cornstarch or potato starchFor dusting
  • Red bean paste (optional)For filling
♡

Helpful tip

Keep some starch nearby. Mochi dough is sticky, and a light dusting helps stop it from sticking to your hands.

Follow along

How to Make Mochi

Take your time and ask an adult to help with hot equipment.

01
🥣

Mix

In a microwave-safe bowl, mix the glutinous rice flour, sugar and water until smooth.

02
♨️

Heat

Cover loosely and microwave in short intervals, stirring between each one, until the dough turns thick, sticky and slightly translucent. Use oven mitts and adult help.

03
✨

Cool & dust

Let the dough cool until safe to handle. Dust a clean surface and your hands with starch.

04
🍡

Shape

Divide the dough into small portions. Flatten each piece, add a little filling if you like, and gently close it.

Kitchen safety: The dough and bowl can be very hot. Get adult help when heating and handling them. Mochi is very chewy, so make small pieces, chew carefully and never swallow it whole.
